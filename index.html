Jan – Apr 2026

  • Designed a spiking network with CSRF-mediated retinal ganglion circuitry, calibrated to a 2D anti-ambipolar phototransistor, with DoG centre-surround and dual-time-constant adaptation layers.

  • Accuracy gains of 4.4%, 10.4% and 27.4% over baseline SNNs on FMNIST, COD10K-v3 and synthetic SCODE: a lightweight retinomorphic alternative to vision transformers and SINet.

Undergraduate research project

Deep reinforcement learning for analog IC topology synthesis

Prof. Imon Mondal, UGP-II, IIT Kanpur
Aug – Nov 2025

  • Built a REINFORCE policy-gradient framework (γ = 0.95) that synthesises CMOS op-amp topologies from a 36-block library, with a 2000× search-space reduction over evolutionary methods.
  • Engineered a hash-accelerated RL environment with typed terminal matching and canonical-state encoding, and resolved three latent flaws in the reference IEEE TCAD implementation.
  • The agent rediscovered the two-stage Miller-compensated OTA in 180 nm CMOS, verified in ngspice at 64.5 dB DC gain, 66.2° phase margin and 12.5 dB gain margin.
